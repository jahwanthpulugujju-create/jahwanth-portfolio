- Page transitions: opacity 0→1, y 20→0, 0.5s ease
- Scroll reveal: use `useInView` hook, each section animates in once on enter, `initial={{ opacity:0, y:40 }}` → `animate={{ opacity:1, y:0 }}`, duration 0.7s
- Stagger children: 0.12s between children in grids
- Hover states on cards: `whileHover={{ y: -6 }}` with spring physics
